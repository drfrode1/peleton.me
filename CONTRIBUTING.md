# Contributing to peleton.me

We welcome contributions from companies, independent developers, and partner organizations[^1]!

## Licensing & Terms
By contributing to this repository, you agree that:
1. Your contributions are licensed under the project's **Apache License 2.0**[^1].
2. You provide your contributions without ongoing maintenance commitments or liabilities.
3. The project name, branding, domain, and official platform governance remain under the sole ownership of **Tour of Norway**.

## Frictionless Workflow
- No complex Contributor License Agreements (CLAs) are required[^2].
- Simply open a Pull Request.
- All contributors and supporting organizations are automatically credited using the **All-Contributors** specification[^2].
